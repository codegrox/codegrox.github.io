# Project 0 — Becoming Friends with Your Camera

CS180, Fall 2026. Perspective, focal length and the centre of projection, measured on
three sets of photographs: a selfie shot the wrong way and the right way, perspective
compression on a facade, and a four-frame dolly zoom down a corridor.

**Live page:** <https://codegrox.github.io/proj0/>

Static HTML — `index.html` is self-contained (styles and scripts inline) and `media/`
holds the photographs, the registered frames and the two animated loops.
